# GTA 6 clipping playbook

Collected from web research on 16 November 2026. Most sources are blogs that sell clipping
tools, so treat numbers as indications, not guarantees. The hook figures come from one
open-source project's analysis of 3,481 gaming Shorts and are title effects, used here as a
proxy for on-screen hooks.

## Moments that work

A viral GTA clip has three parts: a trigger the viewer recognises in the first second, enough
context to follow it, and a human reaction. Look for:

| type | what it looks like |
|---|---|
| Police chase | wanted stars rising, a narrow escape, a chase ending in a crash or a clean getaway |
| Crash or physics | a car launched in the air, ragdoll falls, an explosion chain |
| Glitch or bug | anything the game clearly should not do |
| NPC reaction | a pedestrian or cop doing something unexpectedly smart, dumb or funny |
| Stunt | a jump, a landing, a bike or plane trick that works (or nearly) |
| New feature or detail | something GTA 6 does that GTA 5 did not; small realistic details |
| First time | the player discovering a place, vehicle, weapon or mechanic |
| Fail | a plan that goes wrong fast |
| Roleplay / dialogue | a line or exchange that is funny on its own; captions carry it |
| Streamer reaction | scream, laugh, disbelief; the face is the content, use `split` |

Skip: menus, loading, map screens, long quiet driving, shopping, anything that needs two
minutes of backstory.

## Length

- Reaction or moment: 9-35 s, aim near 22 s.
- Explanation or "X new features": 20-58 s, aim near 45 s.
- Never 60-90 s unless it is a skit with a payoff. A 14 s clip watched to the end beats a 40 s
  clip abandoned halfway.

## Hooks

Effect = views compared with the same channel's usual Shorts.

| hook type | effect | example | use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Number promise | 2.46x | "4 new features coming in GTA 6" | yes |
| Shock fact | 1.68x | "He's 72 years old" | yes |
| Relatable situation | 1.48x | "When the cops spot you at 1 star" | yes |
| Withheld / negation | 1.41x | "Kids can't play GTA 6" | yes |
| Question | 1.24x | "Can you outrun 5 stars on a scooter?" | yes |
| Names a streamer | 1.21x | added to any type | yes, if true |
| Tease | 1.17x | "This cop did not expect that" | yes |
| Superlative | 1.14x | "The fastest car in Vice City" | yes |
| Premise + twist | 0.97x | | neutral |
| First-person claim | 0.94x | | neutral |
| All caps | 0.90x | | avoid |
| Bare label, no structure | 0.88x | "GTA 6 funny moment" | avoid |
| Announcement wording | 0.85x | "New GTA 6 video out now" | avoid |
| Vague | 0.67x | "Wait for it..." | never |

The examples for number promise, shock fact and negation come from the source data; the others
are illustrations written for this playbook.

Rules:

- Never say what it is or how it ends. Name the situation, withhold the outcome.
- End the clip on the result, not on the decision.
- Draft a hook for each type that honestly fits, keep the best. Vary types across a batch.
- The first frame is the thumbnail, the hook and the pitch at once: open mid-action.

## Captions

- 1-3 words at a time, bold, white with a black outline, the spoken word highlighted.
- About 8-10% of the frame height. Most short-form viewing is muted, and RP clips live on dialogue.
- Mask swear words in the text; leave the audio alone.

## Layout

- Gameplay is recorded 16:9, the feeds are 9:16. Do not leave empty black bars.
- Streamer on screen: face on top, game below.
- Keep text out of the top 8% and the bottom 20% of the frame, where the apps draw their own
  buttons and captions (a working margin chosen for this skill, not a published figure).

## What Rockstar allows

Rockstar's general video policy (last updated January 2025, not written for GTA 6) welcomes
gameplay videos. Reported takedown cases:

- any footage from a leaked or early copy before the official release date (19 November 2026);
- the ending, edited cutscene compilations, and other major story reveals, with no stated end date;
- isolated cutscenes. A cutscene inside a longer narrated playthrough is treated differently.

Monetised clips are a grey area: the policy is written for non-commercial fan videos. Platforms
add their own rule: YouTube pays only for clips with real edits or commentary, and reposting
the same footage counts as reused content.

Licensed radio music triggers Content ID claims. Turn the radio off while recording.

## Getting paid

- Clipping campaigns pay per 1,000 verified views. Whop Content Rewards: about $0.20-$6, typical
  $1. Vyro: about $1-$3 depending on the campaign, 5,000 views minimum per post, payout after the
  campaign ends and views are verified.
- No official GTA 6 campaign from Rockstar was found. Campaigns come from streamers and brands
  who want their GTA 6 content clipped. Read each campaign's rules page before cutting.
- Risks reported: budgets running out before views are verified, disputed view counts, payouts
  cancelled for fake views or banned accounts.
